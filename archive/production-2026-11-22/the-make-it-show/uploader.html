The Make It Show Uploader

A personal desktop tool that turns selected OBS clips and completed show recordings into YouTube uploads for Uncle Max, @maxwellflohr.

PrivacyUse of the toolContact

How it works

Clips: The creator presses CLIP in Show Desk. Once the vertical cut is ready, the tool uploads it to the configured YouTube channel.

Episodes: Completed recorded streams can be edited locally to remove break screens and sustained silence, add chapters, and upload the edited episode.

Channel check: The uploader checks the connected channel’s ID before sending video. Automatic publishing can be paused in the local publishing desk.

AI-assisted editing

When enabled, the editor analyzes speech and selected frames to choose an opening, write supported titles, and compose covers from the actual streamer or guest visible in the footage. It preserves synchronized full-width replay footage so a vertical gameplay crop does not lose the webcam. It can add speech captions with anonymous speaker labels, short synchronized reaction insets, and three thumbnail alternatives. Native thumbnail experiments and Shorts related-video links are completed in YouTube Studio. It does not generate or swap faces. Unfinished edits and covers without a usable real face are held for review.

Privacy

Last updated October 25, 2026. This describes the current personal desktop uploader.

The tool uses Google OAuth to request YouTube upload permission and read-only YouTube account access. It reads the channel identifier and basic channel details to confirm the upload destination. It sends the selected video, title, description, and publishing settings to YouTube and reads upload status and processing receipts.

Optional performance learning requests separate read-only YouTube Analytics permission. It reads video engagement, watch time, and retention reports for this channel’s published show videos. Reports stay on the creator’s Mac; they are not sent to OpenAI. Local comparisons of sufficiently sized, similar-length video groups can make small changes to future edit rankings. These are observational comparisons, not guarantees of reach or proof that an edit caused a result. Cached analytics is refreshed or removed within 30 days. Analytics access can be revoked through Google Account connections.

Google access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the creator’s Mac in files restricted to the local user. The tool does not request or store the Google account password. Local upload records contain file references, titles, status, and resulting YouTube video links. The OBS media worker makes local working copies of authorized show media; raw recordings remain in the creator’s archive.

Optional AI editing sends extracted show audio and selected video frames to the OpenAI API. Transcripts, edit decisions, generated covers, and edited working copies are stored locally on the creator’s Mac. Google OAuth tokens are never sent to OpenAI. Responses requests disable response storage; API processing remains subject to OpenAI’s privacy policy. The local dashboard shows whether AI editing is ready or waiting for setup.

The uploader does not sell Google account data or use it for advertising. It does not send Google API data to an AI service. It uses Google API data to verify the channel, perform requested uploads, show publishing status, and provide optional local performance learning. Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

The creator can pause publishing in the local dashboard and revoke Google access through Google Account connections. Local connection and job files remain until removed from the Mac. Revoking access or deleting local files does not delete videos already published to YouTube; those are managed in YouTube Studio. Contact bigmax2010@gmail.com for questions about this personal tool.

Use of the tool

This tool is for the channel owner’s personal use. It is not a public account-registration or video-hosting service. The creator controls the media selected for upload and is responsible for having the rights needed to publish it. YouTube use remains subject to YouTube’s Terms of Service and platform policies.

Publishing depends on valid account authorization, available media, network connectivity, and YouTube processing. The local publishing desk shows completed uploads and items requiring attention.
